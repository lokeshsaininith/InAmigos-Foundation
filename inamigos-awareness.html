Who We Are

A Movement for a Better Tomorrow

InAmigos Foundation is a registered non-profit organisation founded on September 23, 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla. Based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, we operate across multiple Indian states including Delhi, Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, and Madhya Pradesh.

We believe in the power of unity, compassion, and collective action. Our mission is to uplift underprivileged communities through structured, transparent, and impactful programmes — covering education, food, women's empowerment, animal welfare, and environmental protection.

✓ Serving communities across 5+ Indian states
✓ Over 28 dedicated volunteers across India
✓ 6 flagship programmes with measurable impact
✓ 100% transparent — every rupee tracked
✓ Donors eligible for tax exemption (80G)
Our Initiatives

Six Projects, One Mission

Every project addresses a real gap in society — from the plate of a hungry child to the lungs of a dying forest.

Project SEVA

Food & Clothing Distribution

Tackling hunger and inadequate clothing among impoverished communities. Project SEVA provides nutritious meals and essential clothing, fostering dignity and raising awareness about food security so no family sleeps hungry.

🍱 50,000+ meals & clothing distributed
Project BACHPANSHALA

Education for Underprivileged Children

Bridging educational gaps by training underprivileged children in basic digital literacy, life skills, and school education support — ensuring every child gets the future they deserve.

📚 Hundreds of children supported
Project UDAAN

Women Empowerment

Collaborating with self-help groups in rural areas to promote financial independence, skill development, and menstrual hygiene awareness — giving women the wings to soar.

👩 Hundreds of women empowered
Project PRAKRITI

Environmental Conservation

Advocating for sustainability and environmental conservation by planting thousands of saplings and supporting eco-friendly agriculture — for a greener, healthier planet.

🌳 20,000+ saplings planted
Project JEEV

Animal Welfare

Dedicated to the care and protection of stray animals, feeding 50+ animals daily through our volunteer network — because compassion extends to all living beings.

🐾 50+ animals fed daily
Project VIKAS

Skill Development & Employment

Facilitating employment and skill development through internships in data operations, finance, research, content writing, digital marketing, social work, and more across India.

💼 30,000+ interns trained in 4 years
